How-It-Works.txt — Protocol
Operating Instructions

Enrol. Pick trails. Show up.

01
Enrol as a student
Sign up and choose up to three trails that interest you. No screening test, no prior-experience requirement.
02
Follow your trails
Mentors post announcements, session notices, notes, and resources directly to each trail. Pull what's useful, skip what isn't.
03
Learn from mentors, not a syllabus
Sessions are peer-led by students who've been where you are. Ask questions a lecture hall doesn't leave room for.
04
Grow into one
Stick around and know your trail well? Mentors can be promoted from within — Prometheus runs on people who came up through it.
